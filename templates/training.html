Red flags in this simulation

  • Unexpected sender. The message came from {{ template.sender_email }}, not a trusted company domain.
  • Urgency. Phrases like “action required” push you to act before thinking.
  • Generic greeting. “Hi {{ participant.name.split()[0] }}” can be automated and personalized from public data.
  • Lookalike destination. The button led to a page designed to resemble a legitimate portal.
  • Credential request. Legitimate teams rarely ask you to confirm a password through an email link.

What to do next time

  1. Hover over links and check the real destination before clicking.
  2. Open the service directly in your browser instead of using the email link.
  3. Check the sender’s full email address, not just the display name.
  4. Report suspicious messages using your organization’s reporting button.
  5. Never enter a password on a page you reached from an unexpected message.
